Agent-Chromium

Welcome. Four things to set up, once.

This is a browser your coding agents can drive, with your logins. Chromium doesn't let these four settings be preset, so they're yours to pick. Each takes a few seconds, and the page for each is open in the tabs next to this one.

  1. Switch on the two extensions

    Bitwarden and Chromium Web Store are installed but off: Chromium asks once for extensions that didn't come from its store. Flip both switches.

    chrome://extensions

  2. Bring your tabs back after a restart

    Pick Continue where you left off. Agents sometimes have to restart the browser; this way nothing you had open is lost.

    chrome://settings/onStartup

  3. Pick a search engine

    ungoogled-chromium ships without one. DuckDuckGo is quick and doesn't track you.

    chrome://settings/search

  4. Log in to Bitwarden

    Click the shield icon at the top right and log in; your account's region is picked on that screen. Suggested, in Bitwarden's settings: a vault timeout of 15 minutes with the action Lock, and autofill on page load off, so pages your agents visit aren't filled without you.

Two things you may see

A keychain prompt for Chromium Safe Storage: click Always Allow. That's the key protecting your cookies, and the prompt returns after each upgrade of the browser.

A number on the Chromium Web Store icon: extensions you installed from the store have updates waiting. Click it to update.

Done? Quit with ⌘Q or leave the window open; agents open the browser when they need it, and it's the same browser you get from the Dock. This page lives at ~/.agent-chromium/welcome.html.
